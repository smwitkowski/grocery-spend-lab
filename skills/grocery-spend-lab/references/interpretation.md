# Interpretation boundaries

## Coverage

Describe the retailer, loyalty account, date range, receipt count, missing descriptions, excluded zero-received rows, and reconciliation exceptions before household-level conclusions. Reconcile against card or budget transactions when the question requires total spending.

## Discounts

Call a line “retailer-recorded as marked down” when the provider savings field is positive. Do not call it a bargain without a like-for-like unit-price or competitor comparison.

## Price movement

Use exact UPCs and report observation count and date span. Promotions, channel, store, package changes, and household selection remain confounders. Avoid the term inflation for this dataset.

## Product positioning

Price thirds are within-category package-price bands, not normalized unit-price or quality tiers. Organic, convenience, and diet labels are explicit wording signals. They can overlap and do not measure nutrition.

## Changes over time

Separate changes in receipt frequency, basket total, line count, channel mix, product mix, and prices. An event window is descriptive unless the data supports a causal design.
